Magnetic Detector

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Magnetic Detector has no accounts and no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your readings stay on your device. The free version shows advertising, which is explained in detail below.

About this app

Magnetic Detector ("the app") is an app developed by José Belló that reads your device's built-in magnetometer to display magnetic field measurements. This policy explains what happens to data when you use it.

Information we collect ourselves

No personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no registration forms. We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files.

Your readings and settings stay on your device

Saved readings, the place names you type when saving them, measurement history and preferences such as units, sound and display colour are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Device sensors

The app reads your device's built-in magnetometer to measure the surrounding magnetic field. These readings are processed on the device in real time to drive the gauge, the 3D vector view and the alert sounds. They are not transmitted anywhere and are not stored beyond the session unless you choose to save a reading.

Camera

Some visual features overlay magnetic field data on the live camera view using augmented reality. The camera image is processed on your device and displayed on screen only. It is never recorded, uploaded or sent to us or to any third party. The app asks for camera permission the first time you open one of these features, and you can revoke it at any time in the iOS Settings.

The microphone is never used

The app does not listen to your surroundings. The detector sounds you hear are generated from audio files bundled inside the app. The app does not request microphone permission, does not record you, and cannot hear you.

No location data

The app does not request or use your GPS location. When you save a reading, the place name is whatever text you type yourself.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob, including interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and process device information in order to serve and measure those ads, including your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device model, operating system version and general interaction data.

Your choice on tracking

On iOS the app asks for your permission before any tracking identifier is used, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, no advertising identifier is shared and the ads you see will be non-personalised. You can change this decision at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland

The app presents a consent form, provided through Google's User Messaging Platform, before serving personalised advertising. Your choices are respected and can be changed later from within the app.

You can read how Google handles advertising data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Removing the ads

Ads are removed for users with an active subscription or a one-time purchase that includes ad removal. See the Purchases section below.

Usage analytics and crash reports

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the app collects anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics and crash diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics. This includes events such as which screens are opened, how long a feature is used, app version, build number and device model, plus the technical details of any crash.

It does not include your name, your saved readings, or the place names you type. You can read how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase is valid, so it can unlock the corresponding features. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

The app also asks Apple which version of the app you originally downloaded. This is used solely to make sure that users who installed the app under an earlier pricing model keep the access they already had. It is a single technical value; it does not identify you.

You can view, change or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Data retention and security

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we have nothing about you to retain or delete. Content you create stays on your device, under your control. Anonymous analytics, crash and advertising data are retained by Google according to its own policies, described in the links above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. As we hold no personal data about you, most requests can be resolved by deleting the app or the content stored inside it, and by adjusting the tracking and consent settings described above. If you need help finding the right channel, get in touch.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
