Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Spirit Entities Talker has no accounts and no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your sessions, recordings and saved words stay on your device. The app shows no advertising.

About this app

Spirit Entities Talker ("the app") is an entertainment app developed by Bello Studios LLC. This policy explains what happens to data when you use it.

Information we collect ourselves

No personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files.

Your settings and content stay on your device

Session history, saved words, word banks, recordings and preferences such as band, sweep speed and display colour are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes them.

The microphone is never used

Despite what the app simulates, it does not listen to your surroundings. All audio is generated from sound files bundled inside the app. The app does not request microphone permission, does not record you, and cannot hear you.

Session recordings are made from the audio the app itself produces. They are written to your device's storage and are never sent to us or to any third party. You decide whether to keep, delete or share them.

Device sensors

The EMF features read your device's built-in magnetometer, and some visual features use the camera. These readings are processed on the device in real time to drive what you see on screen. They are not transmitted anywhere and are not stored beyond the session unless you save it yourself.

Usage analytics

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the app collects anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics. This includes events such as which screens are opened, how long a feature is used, and app version and device model. It does not include your name, your recordings, or the content of your sessions, and it is not used to build an advertising profile.

You can read how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The app contains no advertising. No advertising identifier is collected and no ad network receives data from the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase is valid, so it can unlock the corresponding features. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

You can view, change or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Data retention and security

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we have nothing about you to retain or delete. Content you create stays on your device, under your control. Anonymous analytics data is retained by Google according to its own policies, described in the link above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. As we hold no personal data about you, most requests can be resolved by deleting the app or the content stored inside it. If you need help finding the right channel, get in touch.

Changes to this policy
