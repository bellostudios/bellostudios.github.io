Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Short version: the app does not ask for your name, your email, or any account. It does not record audio, use your microphone, or track your location. The only data leaving your device is what the Google advertising system needs in order to show ads.

Spirit Box app

SBX-14 Spirit Box ("the app") is the softwre developed. This policy explains what happens to data when you use the app.

Information we collect ourselves

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms, and no analytics of our own. We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or files.

Your settings stay on your device

Choices such as band, static level, display colour and sweep speed are held only while the app is running or stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded.

The microphone is never used

Despite what the app simulates, it does not listen to your surroundings. All audio is generated from sound files bundled inside the app. The app does not request microphone permission, does not record anything, and cannot hear you.

Advertising

The app is free and is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Ads are shown full-screen between sessions only — never while the box is scanning.

To deliver, measure and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may process:

  • a resettable advertising identifier associated with your device;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and clicks.

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller. You can read how Google uses this information in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message the first time you open it, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You choose there whether ads may be personalised.

You can change that choice at any time: open the app, tap the info button on the main screen, and use Privacy choices at the bottom of the guide.

Turning off personalised ads on your device

Android also lets you limit ad personalisation system-wide, or reset your advertising ID, from Settings → Google → Ads. Doing so does not remove ads; it makes them less relevant.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will look into it.

Data retention and security

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we have nothing about you to retain or delete. Data processed for advertising is retained by Google according to its own policies, which are described in the links above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. As we hold no personal data about you, requests concerning advertising data should be directed to Google. We are happy to help you find the right channel — just get in touch.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
