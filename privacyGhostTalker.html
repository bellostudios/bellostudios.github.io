Ghost Talker Live Radio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Ghost Talker Live Radio has no accounts and no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your recordings and settings stay on your device. The free version shows advertising, which is removed by any purchase.

About this app

Ghost Talker Live Radio ("the app") is an entertainment app developed by BelloStudios LLC. This policy explains what happens to data when you use it.

Information we collect ourselves

No personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts or files.

Your settings and content stay on your device

Recordings, the word history of Ghost Talker, and preferences such as sweep speed, display colour or selected tracking model are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes them.

The tools and what they use

Camera — SLS Vision and Thermal Vision

These two tools need the camera, and iOS asks for your permission before either of them can open. The video is processed on the device, in real time, to draw the skeletal tracking overlay or the thermal colour map. Nothing is streamed to us or to anyone else.

If you record a session, the video file is written to your device's storage. It stays there, under your control: you decide whether to keep it, delete it or share it. If a recording includes sound, iOS will also ask for microphone permission the first time.

Spirit Box — live internet radio

The Spirit Box tunes real radio stations streamed over the internet. That means your device connects directly to the servers of those stations, and, as with any internet connection, they can see your IP address and technical details of the request. We do not control those servers and we do not receive anything from them. Their own privacy practices are their own.

We do not log which stations you listen to.

Ghost Talker — words and speech

The words are drawn at random from a list bundled inside the app and spoken aloud with the speech voices that come with iOS. The app does not listen to your surroundings to produce them, and the words it shows are not sent anywhere.

EMF Meter — device sensors

The EMF features read your device's built-in magnetometer. Those readings are processed on the device to drive what you see on screen. They are not transmitted anywhere and are not stored once you leave the tool.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob receives technical information from your device, such as device model, iOS version, language, approximate region and identifiers used to measure and cap ads.

Tracking permission

On iOS the app asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites, using your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you decline, the identifier is not used and you will see non-personalised ads instead. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in Europe or the UK

Before any ad is requested, the app shows a consent form managed by Google's User Messaging Platform, where you choose which purposes you accept. Your choice is respected for every ad request afterwards.

You can read how Google handles advertising data in Google's advertising policy.

Removing ads

Any purchase removes the advertising entirely. From that point the app makes no ad requests and no ad network receives anything from it.

Usage analytics

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the app collects anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics. This includes events such as which tools are opened, how long a tool is used, whether a purchase screen was shown, and app version and device model. It does not include your name, your recordings, or the images from your camera, and we do not use it to identify you.

You can read how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase is valid, so it can unlock the corresponding features and switch the advertising off. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

To restore a purchase on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app's menu. You can view, change or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Data retention and security

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we have nothing about you to retain or delete. Content you create stays on your device, under your control. Anonymous analytics data and advertising data are retained by Google according to its own policies, described in the links above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. As we hold no personal data about you, most requests can be resolved by deleting the app or the content stored inside it, by withdrawing tracking permission in iOS Settings, or by changing your consent choices in the app. If you need help finding the right channel, get in touch.
